Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about enrollment and our school.

What is the admission process?

Submit the registration form, attend an interview, and provide previous academic records. Our admissions team will contact you within 3 business days.

What are the school fees?

School fees vary by grade. Please contact our office for detailed fee structures and payment plans available.

Do you offer boarding?

Yes, we offer both day and boarding options. Boarding facilities are available for students from Senior 1 and above.

What extracurricular activities do you offer?

We offer sports, music, drama, art, debate club, science club, and community service programs.

What are the school hours?

School runs from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM on weekdays. Boarding students have evening study sessions and activities.

How can I schedule a campus visit?

Use the contact form above or call our office directly to schedule a personalized tour of our campus.
