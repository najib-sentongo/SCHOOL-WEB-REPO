About Light Academy

Discover our story, mission, and the values that drive us forward.

Our Mission

At Light Academy, our mission is to provide holistic education that empowers students to become critical thinkers, responsible citizens, and lifelong learners. We are committed to nurturing each student's unique talents while fostering a culture of excellence, integrity, and innovation.

We strive to create an inclusive learning environment where every student feels valued and supported in their academic journey.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be a leading educational institution that transforms lives through quality education. We aim to produce graduates who are not only academically proficient but also morally upright and ready to contribute meaningfully to society.

We envision a future where every student from Light Academy becomes a catalyst for positive change in their community and beyond.

Our History

A journey of growth and achievement since 2010.

2010 - The Beginning

Light Academy was founded with a vision to provide quality education in the Kigo area. Starting with just 50 students and 5 teachers, we began our journey towards educational excellence.

2015 - Expansion

We expanded our campus facilities, adding new science laboratories, a library, and sports facilities. Student enrollment grew to over 200 students.

2020 - Recognition

Light Academy received recognition for academic excellence from the Ministry of Education. Our students achieved outstanding results in national examinations.

2026 - Present Day

Today, Light Academy serves over 500 students with a team of 50+ dedicated teachers. We continue to innovate and adapt to provide the best possible education for our students.
